# AI Prompt Skeleton — free sample

Five prompts built on one skeleton, extracted from a 28-product catalog of
documentation-shaped AI business assets.

```
Role / Task / Input / Format / Tone / Do-NOT
```

The `Do-NOT` block is what stops generic output. Without it, a prompt is a wish.

## 1. Cold email that doesn't read like AI

**Role:** B2B copywriter for a small service business.
**Task:** Write a first-touch cold email to a prospect who never replied.
**Input:** {{company}}, {{what they do}}, {{one specific observation}}, {{offer}}.
**Format:** Subject line + 90-word body + one-line CTA. Plain text.
**Tone:** Peer, direct, no hype.
**Do-NOT:** No "I hope this email finds you well", no "delve/leverage/elevate", no bullet lists, no more than one question.

## 2. Meeting notes to decisions

**Role:** Chief of staff.
**Task:** Convert raw meeting notes into decisions, owners and dates.
**Input:** {{notes}}.
**Format:** Table: Decision | Owner | Due | Blocker. Then "Open questions" max 3.
**Tone:** Neutral.
**Do-NOT:** Do not invent owners or dates. Mark unknowns as `TBD`.

## 3. Proposal scope that prevents scope creep

**Role:** Agency lead writing a scope of work.
**Task:** Turn a client brief into in-scope / out-of-scope / change-request rules.
**Input:** {{brief}}.
**Format:** Three sections + a 5-row pricing table with unit.
**Tone:** Plain, contractual.
**Do-NOT:** Do not promise revisions, timelines for third-party approvals, or usage rights not stated in the brief.

## 4. Support reply for an angry customer

**Role:** Support lead.
**Task:** Reply to a complaint about a late delivery.
**Input:** {{complaint}}, {{what actually happened}}, {{remedy you can offer}}.
**Format:** 3 short paragraphs: acknowledge → fact → remedy + next step.
**Tone:** Calm, accountable.
**Do-NOT:** Do not blame the customer, do not offer refunds not approved, no emojis, no "we apologize for any inconvenience".

## 5. SOP from a process you do by hand

**Role:** Operations consultant.
**Task:** Turn a described manual process into a runbook.
**Input:** {{process description}}.
**Format:** Purpose / Triggers / Inputs / Steps / Decision points / Failure handling / Output.
**Tone:** Imperative.
**Do-NOT:** Do not skip failure handling. If a step depends on a tool, name the tool.
